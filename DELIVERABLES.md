## 💡 Key Decisions Made

### Technology Decisions
- ✅ React Native (cross-platform mobile)
- ✅ Expo (rapid development)
- ✅ Node.js + Express (backend API)
- ✅ PostgreSQL (database)
- ✅ Next.js 14 (admin panel)
- ✅ Redux Toolkit (state management)
- ✅ Prisma (ORM)
- ✅ OpenAI/Claude (AI assistant)
- ✅ ElevenLabs (voice generation)
- ✅ Razorpay/Stripe (payments)

### Design Decisions
- ✅ Crystal white + ice blue aesthetic
- ✅ Glassmorphism design language
- ✅ Premium, peaceful atmosphere
- ✅ Apple-quality polish
- ✅ 3D glass icon bubbles

### Business Decisions
- ✅ Freemium model (free + premium)
- ✅ Monthly, Yearly, and Family plans
- ✅ 7-day free trial
- ✅ Generous free tier
- ✅ Premium features add real value

### Feature Decisions
- ✅ No separate Prayer section
- ✅ No generic community feed
- ✅ User hymns default to private
- ✅ AI answers must cite Scripture
- ✅ Family plan members fully private
- ✅ Admin panel owner-only
