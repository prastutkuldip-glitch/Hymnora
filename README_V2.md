## 📱 What is HYMNORA?

HYMNORA is a **premium Christian faith application** that brings the Bible, worship, and devotion together in a beautiful, modern interface. Experience Scripture, hymns, and spiritual growth tools designed with care for believers worldwide.

### 🎯 Mission Statement
To create the most comprehensive, user-friendly, and spiritually enriching digital companion for Christians, making Scripture and worship accessible, engaging, and meaningful for daily faith practice.

### 📖 **Complete Bible Chapters** (100+ Verses)
- **John Chapter 3** (21 verses) - "For God so loved the world..."
- **Psalms 23** (6 verses) - "The LORD is my shepherd..."

- **Philippians 4:6-13** - "I can do all things through Christ..."

**Available in 3 Languages:**

5. **It Is Well With My Soul** (1873) - Horatio G. Spafford

### 🌟 **Interactive Features**

#### 📝 Personal Notes System
- Add personal reflections to any verse
- Beautiful yellow-highlighted note cards
- Edit or delete notes anytime
- Perfect for Bible study and devotions

#### 🔖 Bookmark & Highlight
- **Bookmark** your favorite verses for quick access
- **Highlight** important passages with yellow background
- Instant visual feedback with bubble design

#### 📤 WhatsApp Share
- Share verses directly to WhatsApp
- Formatted with book, chapter, verse reference
- "Shared via HYMNORA" attribution
- Perfect for encouraging friends and family

#### 🌅 Daily Verse Rotation
- **7 inspiring verses** rotate automatically
- Changes daily based on day of year
- Features: John 3:16, Psalm 23:1, Philippians 4:13, Romans 8:28, and more
- Never miss your daily inspiration

#### 🕐 Dynamic Greetings
Time-based personalized greetings:
- ☀️ **Morning** (5am-12pm): "Good Morning ☀️ - May the Lord bless your day"
- 🌤️ **Afternoon** (12pm-5pm): "Good Afternoon 🌤️ - Continue in His grace"
- 🌆 **Evening** (5pm-9pm): "Good Evening 🌆 - Find peace in His presence"
- 🌙 **Night** (9pm-5am): "Good Night 🌙 - Rest in His promises"

### 👁️ **Dual Reading Modes**
- **Verse-by-Verse View**: Traditional reading with numbered verses
- **Paragraph View**: Flowing text for natural reading experience
- Toggle instantly with one click

## 🎨 Design Philosophy

### Premium Bubble Glassmorphism UI
**Color Palette:**
- **White**: 60% - Primary background (#FFFFFF, #F7FBFE, #EFF6FD)
- **Crystal Blue**: 30% - Accent color (#4A9FFF, #2B7FE6)
- **Dark Navy**: 10% - Text and contrast (#0C1E35, #1A1A1A)

**Design Elements:**
- Bubble glassmorphism icons with shine animations
- Smooth gradients and backdrop blur effects
- Rounded corners (12px-24px) for friendly feel
- Elevated shadows for depth perception
- Mobile-first responsive design

**Navigation:**
- Bottom navigation with animated bubble icons
- Smooth screen transitions
- Easy one-thumb operation
- Clear visual hierarchy
