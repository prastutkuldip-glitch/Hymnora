## Version 2.0 - Enhanced Edition (September 23, 2026)

### 🎉 Major Features Added

#### 📖 New Bible Content
- **Romans 8** - Complete chapter with all 39 verses (KJV)
  - Full English translation with accurate KJV text
  - Hindi translation with proper scripture formatting
  - Assamese translation for regional accessibility
  - Includes powerful verses like Romans 8:28, 8:31, 8:37-39

- **Philippians 4:6-13** - Inspirational verses
  - "I can do all things through Christ" (4:13)
  - Peace and contentment teachings
  - Available in all 3 languages

#### 🎵 New Hymns (4 Added)
1. **Blessed Assurance** - Fanny J. Crosby (1873)
   - 3 verses + chorus
   - "This is my story, this is my song"

2. **It Is Well With My Soul** - Horatio G. Spafford (1873)
   - 4 verses + chorus
   - Historical hymn of faith through tragedy

3. **In Christ Alone** - Keith Getty & Stuart Townend (2001)
   - 4 complete verses
   - Modern worship classic

4. **Holy Holy Holy** - (Already included)

**Total Hymns: 7** (Amazing Grace, How Great Thou Art, Holy Holy Holy, Blessed Assurance, It Is Well, In Christ Alone, plus original hymns)

#### ✨ New Interactive Features

##### 🌅 Daily Verse Rotation
- 7 inspiring verses that rotate based on the day of year
- Includes John 3:16, Psalm 23:1, Philippians 4:13, Romans 8:28, Romans 8:1, and more
- Automatic daily change at midnight

##### 📝 Verse Notes System
- Add personal notes to any Bible verse
- Notes displayed in attractive yellow-highlighted cards
- Edit or delete notes anytime
- Notes persist during session

##### 🕐 Dynamic Greetings
- Time-based greetings change throughout the day:
  - Morning (5am-12pm): "Good Morning ☀️"
  - Afternoon (12pm-5pm): "Good Afternoon 🌤️"
  - Evening (5pm-9pm): "Good Evening 🌆"
  - Night (9pm-5am): "Good Night 🌙"
- Contextual messages: "May the Lord bless your day", "Continue in His grace", etc.
